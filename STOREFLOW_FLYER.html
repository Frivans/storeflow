Engineered for High-Velocity Retail & Wholesale

Built to handle the real-world operational challenges of busy storefronts and supply depots.

Multi-Tier Packaging (UOM)

Sell by the piece, crate, or carton with automatic stock breakdown ($14 \text{ crates} \times 5 \text{ boxes} = 70 \text{ crates}$). Zero manual math errors at checkout.

Built-in Barcode Studio

Generate and print compliant EAN-13, Code-128, and custom retail shelf tags directly on standard thermal sticker rolls or A4 label paper.

Zero-Profit Restock Returns

Accountant-grade refunds. Restocked units restore FIFO valuation; damaged items route to verifiable loss ledgers without distorting sales margins.

FEFO Perishable Tracking

First-Expired, First-Out alerts for food, eggs, dairy, and pharmaceuticals. Avoid unsellable expired inventory before it reaches store shelves.

Shift Audits & Cash Float

Record opening float, track cash vs. mobile money payouts in real time, and generate tamper-evident end-of-day cashier balancing receipts.

Automated 2-Hour Backups

Never lose a transaction to power outages or PC crashes. Encrypted ZIP archives are generated every 2 hours with instant 1-click ledger restore.

100% Plug-and-Play Hardware Ready
Works with standard 58mm/80mm ESC/POS thermal printers, USB handheld barcode guns, cash drawers, and customer displays.
