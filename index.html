Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection to use StoreFlow?

No! StoreFlow is 100% offline-first. All data is saved directly on your local computer or phone. You can make sales, print receipts, and balance shifts even with zero internet or network.

Can I use my Android phone instead of a laptop?

Yes. We provide a native standalone Android APK. You can use your phone camera to scan barcodes and connect via Bluetooth to portable receipt printers.

What happens if my computer crashes or gets stolen?

StoreFlow features automated backup encryption. Copies of your books are stored safely in your Documents folder, and can be backed up to a USB flash drive or your cloud drive with one click.
